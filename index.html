Our story

Tarang started on a kitchen table with a spool of waxed cord and a box of brass beads. What began as pieces made for friends slowly became a small studio practice — still hand-strung, still finished one bracelet at a time, just with a few more hands helping now. We keep batches small on purpose: it's the only way we know to keep the quality where we want it.
